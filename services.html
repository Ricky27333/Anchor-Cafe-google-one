Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning your beach experience with Anchor Cafe at Ramapuram Beach, Chirala.

How far in advance should I book? +

For Beach Campfires and Night Tables, we recommend booking at least 12 to 24 hours in advance so our beach crew can secure prime sand spots and prepare fresh firewood. For Grand Birthday Event Lightings, booking 2 to 3 days in advance is advised for custom marquee and pergola decor.

What happens if weather or high tide changes? +

Safety is our highest priority. In the rare event of unseasonal rain or rough wind, we immediately provide covered panoramic seating on our elevated cafe deck terrace or offer full flexibility to reschedule your session at no extra charge.

Can we order our favorite food & drinks from the cafe menu? +

Absolutely! All beach experiences include seamless table/beach service from Anchor Cafe’s full menu — including single-origin coffees, handcrafted mojitos, thick Belgian milkshakes, artisanal ice creams, french fries, crispy momos, and hot sandwiches.

Are beach sports (cricket & volleyball) suitable for beginners? +

Yes! Our beach volleyball court and beach cricket turf are set up on soft, level sands suitable for all ages and skill levels. We provide tennis balls and light bats for safe, casual beach fun as well as tournament-standard gear for serious matches.
