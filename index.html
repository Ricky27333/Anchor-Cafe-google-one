The Coromandel Narrative

Brewed with Passion on the Choramandel Shoreline

Derived from the ancient Cholamandalam maritime legacy, the Coromandel Coast of Chirala is legendary for its vast golden sands, warm ocean breezes, and peaceful morning waves.

Nestled directly on Ramapuram Beach, Anchor Cafe was created as a coastal sanctuary to slow down time. Whether catching the morning sun with a single-origin pour-over, cooling down with fresh crushed-mint mojitos and Belgian chocolate milkshakes, or sharing savory snacks and artisanal ice creams as dusk sets over the Bay of Bengal, every moment is crafted to perfection.
