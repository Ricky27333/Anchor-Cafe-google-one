The Coastal Narrative

Brewed with Passion on the Shoreline of Chirala

Born from a love for slow mornings, crashing waves, and uncompromising coffee craftsmanship, Anchor Cafe is Chirala’s quintessential coastal sanctuary.

Nestled right on the tranquil sands of Ramapuram Beach, we designed a space that slows down time. Whether you are catching the golden sunrise with an Ethiopian pour-over, cooling down with our crushed-mint passionfruit mojitos, or indulging in wood-fired sourdough bites as dusk falls, every moment is crafted to feel like an effortless weekend getaway.

The Anchor Standard

Crafted for Coastal Unwinding

Four distinct pillars that make our beachfront venue Chirala's premier destination for friends, families, and solo explorers.

Direct Ocean Panorama

Unobstructed 180-degree view of Ramapuram Beach. Feel the refreshing sea breeze on our open teak deck as you sip your morning roast.

Golden Hour Sunset Sessions

As dusk falls over the Bay of Bengal, our ambient warm pendant lighting and relaxed coastal lounge playlists create an unforgettable atmosphere.

Micro-Lot Coffee Roasts

Sourced ethically from shaded Southern Indian estates. Precision ground and pulled on high-end espresso machinery for velvet microfoam and rich crema.

Your furry friends are family. Enjoy our shaded patio equipped with water bowls, spacious seating, and dedicated seaside parking.
